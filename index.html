About

I'm a CSE student at ULAB in Dhaka. I like building things that sit somewhere between code, hardware and art.

I've been drawing for as long as I can remember, and the rest I picked up on my own. I build AI web apps, design wheels for a line follower robot, work on FPGA and digital logic with VHDL, and make hand-painted acrylics, pencil and ink drawings, and clay figures. I also stream games as Aastha Respawns and take part in as many hackathons as I can.

Projects

  • Orbit Split: type a bill in plain words and Gemini splits it. Everyone pays through PayPal sandbox, and a rocket launches when the last person pays. Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon.
  • Keystone: an AI hobby planner that tells beginners what to buy before they start, so they don't waste money. Built solo for the Ostad YUNet AIgnite Buildathon 2026.
  • Arete: a YouTube course tracker where each course orbits as a planet around your progress.

What I'm building and learning

  • Building: a Finance Tracker for logging income and expenses, and the Enceladus art portfolio site.
  • Learning: front-end web development (HTML, CSS, JavaScript), robotics with a line follower robot and wheel design in FreeCAD, and digital logic and FPGA with VHDL.
  • Tech I use: Java, C, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Gemini API, Netlify, Git, ESP32, Arduino, KiCad, Canva and Clip Studio Paint.
